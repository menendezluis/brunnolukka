BIOGRAPHY

Brunno Lukka is a DJ and producer focused on Tech House and Hard Groove for the dance floor. His sets combine CDJs and live drumpad, blending Latin grooves with modern electronic production, built for clubs and late-night energy.

Based in Guatemala City, Brunno is available worldwide for bookings and is developing his project through consistent releases, DJ sets, and a growing online audience. His music is driven by groove, bass, and vocal hooks that connect quickly with listeners.

Whether playing in intimate venues or larger stages, the goal is always the same: keep the groove moving.
